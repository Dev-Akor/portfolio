## Overview

This site is AkorLabs Technologies' company website, blog and case-study platform. It's built for long-term use: content lives in MDX files alongside the code, is validated against a schema at build time, and is rendered to static pages.

## How it works

- **Content as code** — projects and posts are MDX files with typed frontmatter. A missing field fails the build instead of breaking a page.
- **Case studies** — each project page is assembled from its frontmatter (role, timeline, stack, metrics, highlights) plus custom MDX components for architecture diagrams and engineering challenges.
- **Private by design** — most of our work lives in private repositories or is under client NDA. Projects declare their visibility, and private ones show a "request a walkthrough" option instead of a repository link.
- **SEO** — per-page metadata and social cards, JSON-LD structured data, a sitemap, RSS and canonical URLs.
- **Contact** — validated on both client and server, with a notification and an automatic reply sent as transactional email.

## Stack

Next.js App Router with static generation, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS with a small design-token layer, Contentlayer for MDX, and Vercel for hosting. The source is [public on GitHub](https://github.com/dev-akor/portfolio).
